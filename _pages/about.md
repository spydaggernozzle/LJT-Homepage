I am a first-year PhD candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**. My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. My research interests include **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**, **hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)**, and **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**. I am supervised by **Professor Junxian He**.
